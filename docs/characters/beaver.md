## Description

Builds walls.

## Abilities

- **Flood** (active): completing a 3-point wall pushes back your first point in front of it.
- **Gnaw** (passive): while in a wall, pushes back your first blot in front of it 1 pip each turn.

Full rules on the [biome page](../biomes/sloth.md#minion-1-beaver).
