## Description

The slow, defensive, clumsy busboy. Builds walls (fits the Primer / Cautious personalities); his mistakes cause you problems.

## Abilities

- **Butterfingers orders:** drop dirty plates that block your orders.
- **Spill orders:** when refired, put dirty plates on both neighbouring points.

Full rules on the [biome page](../biomes/sloth.md#boss-the-sloth).
