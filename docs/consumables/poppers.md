## Effect

Your next roll is a double. The value is random, each of 1 to 6 equally likely.

Buff: used on your own turn. Family: **More dice or exact dice**.

## Cost & availability

Legendary: 9 tips at the [dish pit](../shops.md#dish-pit).

## Design notes

- Placeholder name.
- Dice roll at the start of every turn, so it always sets the roll of your next turn.
- Worth 14 pips on average, against about 8.2 for a normal roll.
