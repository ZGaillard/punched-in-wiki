## Effect

The enemy plays with one die on its next turn, so it can't roll doubles.

Debuff: used against the enemy. Family: **Take away an enemy die**.

## Cost & availability

Legendary: 9 tips at the [dish pit](../shops.md#dish-pit).

## Design notes

- Easy for the AI to understand.
