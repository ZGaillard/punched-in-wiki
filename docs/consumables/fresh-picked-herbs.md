## Effect

Reroll one or more of your unplayed dice.

Buff: used on your own turn. Family: **Reroll dice**.

## Cost & availability

Rare: 6 tips at the [dish pit](../shops.md#dish-pit).

## Design notes

- Part of the "fine herbs" family.
- Dice changed by a consumable that end up matching count as doubles.
