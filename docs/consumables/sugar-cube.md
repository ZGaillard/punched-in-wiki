## Effect

See your next 3 rolls and the enemy's next 3. Both visions count down 3, 2, 1, 0 as the rolls are played.

Buff: used on your own turn. Family: **See future rolls**.

## Cost & availability

Legendary: 9 coins at the [consumable shop](../shops.md#consumable-shop).
