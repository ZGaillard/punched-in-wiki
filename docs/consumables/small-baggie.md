## Effect

Roll a third die for one turn. A matching pair plays as a normal double; triples give six moves.

Buff: used on your own turn. Family: **More dice or exact dice**.

## Cost & availability

Legendary: 9 tips at the [dish pit](../shops.md#dish-pit).
