## Effect

An extra move on top of your roll: 3 pips split however you like among your orders, each forward or backward.

Buff: used on your own turn. Family: **Extra pips**.

## Cost & availability

Legendary: 9 tips at the [dish pit](../shops.md#dish-pit).

## Design notes

- Part of the cheap nicotine family.
- The AI move generator must support backward moves.
