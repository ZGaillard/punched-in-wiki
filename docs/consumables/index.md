# Consumables

Every consumable: single-use items sold at the [dish pit](../shops.md#dish-pit). Use the filters to narrow the list; the filtered view can be shared by copying the page's URL.

Each consumable is a drug or substance, hinted at but never named. They come
in two sides: **buffs** improve your own turn, **debuffs** hurt the enemy's.
Every entry is tagged with its side (`buff` or `debuff`) and its family (e.g.
`add-to-dice`, `extra-pips`); see the [Tags](../tags.md) page.

**Rule:** dice changed by a consumable that end up matching count as doubles.

The goal is more commons than rares, and more rares than legendaries (currently
10 common, 9 rare, 7 legendary).

To add an entry, copy `_templates/consumable.md` into `docs/consumables/` and fill in its
frontmatter; it appears below automatically.
