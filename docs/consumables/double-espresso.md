## Effect

+1 to both of your dice.

Buff: used on your own turn. Family: **Add to dice**.

## Cost & availability

Rare: 6 coins at the [consumable shop](../shops.md#consumable-shop).

## Design notes

- Dice changed by a consumable that end up matching count as doubles.
