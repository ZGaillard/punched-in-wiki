## Effect

+1 to one of your dice. A 6 can become a 7.

Buff: used on your own turn. Family: **Add to dice**.

## Cost & availability

Common: 4 coins at the [consumable shop](../shops.md#consumable-shop).

## Design notes

- Dice changed by a consumable that end up matching count as doubles.
