# Keywords

Every keyword used in the texts of menu items, consumables, relics and enemies, with its definition. Keywords are listed alphabetically. The moments an effect reacts to ("when refired", "when serving"…) are listed separately in [Triggers](../rules/triggers.md).

To add an entry, copy `_templates/keyword.md` into `docs/keywords/` and fill in its
frontmatter; it appears below automatically.
