## Effect

When it hits, you get a free extra 1-pip move.

## Interactions

The extra pip is optional and can move any of your checkers. A hit made with it earns another.

## Design notes

Your side's version of the Shark's [Blood in the water](../biomes/shark.md). Picked on 2026-11-11 from a brainstorm of simple items that need no new trigger.
