## Effect

When it's hit, the checker that hit it goes to the bar too.

## Interactions

Every time, unlike the Pufferfish, which only does it when it didn't move during its last turn.

## Design notes

Your side's version of the Pufferfish's Puffed up ([The Shark's biome](../biomes/shark.md)). Picked on 2026-11-11 from a brainstorm of simple items that need no new trigger.
