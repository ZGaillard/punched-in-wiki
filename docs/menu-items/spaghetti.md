## Effect

The checker it hits is tangled: it waits 1 turn before going to the bar.

## Interactions

The tangled checker misses its owner's next turn, then goes to the bar.

## Design notes

Your side's version of the Pelican's Numbness ([The Shark's biome](../biomes/shark.md)). Picked on 2026-11-11 from a brainstorm of simple items that need no new trigger.
