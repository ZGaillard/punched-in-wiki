## Effect

In a race (no contact left), it moves 1 extra pip.

## Interactions

Forced, like Fries. Entering from the bar is a normal move.

## Design notes

A late-game item: it does nothing while the checkers are still in contact. Picked on 2026-11-11 from a brainstorm of simple items that need no new trigger.
