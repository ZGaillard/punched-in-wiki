# Decisions

The decision log: every design decision, when it was made and by whom, newest first. Superseded decisions stay in the log, struck through, with a link to the decision that replaced them. Questions not decided yet live in [open questions](open-questions.md).

To add an entry, copy `_templates/decision.md` into `docs/decisions/` and fill in its
frontmatter; it appears below automatically.
