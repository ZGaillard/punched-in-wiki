## Context

[Decision 0002](0002-tips-and-prices.md) gave the player 3 pockets, so at
most 3 consumables at once. Consumables are meant to be stocked up and
combined, and holding several copies of the same one is part of that.

## Decision

- **No pocket limit**: the player can hold any number of consumables,
  including several copies of the same one. This replaces the 3 pockets of
  decision 0002.
- Each card in a dish pit offer still **sells once**. More copies come from the
  same consumable turning up again in a later offer.

## Alternatives considered

- A higher limit (e.g. 6 or 10).
- Cards that can be bought again and again while the player has tips.

## Consequences

Tips become the only limit on consumables, so prices and tip income carry the
balance. Several copies of a waiting effect can stack on the same enemy roll
(see open questions on stacking).
